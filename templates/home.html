Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Our 1-on-1 Career Consultation is a personalized mentoring session with senior industry professionals. We analyze your career goals, audit your resume and portfolio, recommend customized learning roadmaps, and offer actionable advice for career transitions or high-growth tech roles.

Career consultation is strictly exclusive to registered students of Edvanto. If you already have an account on Edvanto, you are fully eligible to request a consultation at zero cost. If not, you must register as a student before booking.

Sessions are held live online via Google Meet or Zoom. Once you submit your preferred date and time, our academic counselors will review your profile and confirm the meeting schedule within 24 business hours.

You can ask about curriculum choices, tech stack comparisons (e.g. Full-Stack vs AI/Data Science vs UI/UX), project portfolio guidance, interview preparation strategies, salary negotiation insights, and industry expectations.
